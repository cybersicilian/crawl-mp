Name collisions & watch-outs

Where a port would collide with content already in our tree, or needs extra care.

Collisions

  • Oni ×3 designs — ours (forgecraft/shapeshifting), bloatcrawl2's (level-up spell discovery, no schools), bcrawl's (random spell gifts every 2 XL, Spellcasting-only). Pick one design or rename the imports.
  • Reaver — ours is a spell-sword; bcrawl's is a melee start worshipping Vehumet. Same slot, different job.
  • Archaeologist ×2 — bloatcrawl2's: dusty tome + ancient crate → random unrandart quest. bcrawl's: whip/curare + acquirement at XL 5. Different jobs wearing one name.
  • Shapeshifter — our job vs bloatcrawl2's species. Alchemist / Paladin job enums exist locally with different designs.
  • Branch WIP overlap — our tree already restructures the mid-game (Lair → Burrows → Wormlands, a 2-floor Hive off Burrows:2, a revived rune-Forest off Vaults on the vestigial BRANCH_FOREST enum, Hall of Blades). This cut two ports: bcrawl's Spriggan Forest (same enum, same forest.des path, near-identical cast) and kimjoy's Hive (same theme, BRANCH_HIVE taken). Their best pieces become feedstock for ours.

Porting terrain

  • bloatcrawl2 species are yaml in the same species-gen.py format we use — the easiest source to port from.
  • kimjoy runs pre-0.26 systems (old job/item enums, old mon-data, retained hunger) — its ports need rebasing onto our modern yaml/enums, not git cherry-picks.
  • Already adopted by trunk (excluded from the lists): rockslime, wendigo, goliath frog, kobold brigand, tile_show_threat_levels.
  • Tone flags — bloatcrawl2 mixes serious designs with memes; every entry below is tagged joke where it applies.

The curated set

The pared-down manifest: one design per niche, collisions rebranded against our tree, jokes cut. Full reasoning and the cut list are in port-manifest.md.

Species — 13 + the undead modifier

  2. Hermit Crab (bc2; absorbs kj Crustacean's molt), Protean (bc2), Faerie Dragon (bc2; absorbs bc Fairy)

Backgrounds — the Elder Pantheon + 12 standalones

Okawaru, Kiku, and Chei are "silent elders" — in the pantheon, but altars-only. Xom / Ignis / Vorr / Yred / Lugonu starts stay as non-elder special cases

Standalones: Hermit (pre-sacrificed to Ru), Slime Priest (pre-mutated), Archaeologist (bc2 crate/tome quest + bc whip/curare kit, merged), Caravan + Collector (kj economies), Rogue (bc), Fencer (bc2), Ronin (bc2 first-god piety), Apothecary (bc2 "Alchemist" renamed — ours is taken), Billionaire, Neckbeard, Philosopher (bc2, kept by request)

Branches & mechanics + build order

  1. Our WIP already has Burrows → Wormlands + Hive, a revived rune-Forest, and Hall of Blades — so Spriggan Forest and kimjoy's Hive are cut; their salvage becomes feedstock (greenwood witch and forest vaults for our Forest, killer bee larvae for our Hive)

  3. Game-modifier framework (undead-any, no-rune-locks, traps, XP) · bc2 yaml plumbing · set-collection + mercenary economies · M&F corpse knockback · bcrawl QoL trio
