# Crawl MP: Changes from Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup

*Draft release notes. The first sections describe features found in the current
working source; the final section previews the remaining curated content port.
The current source has not been verified as a single published build.*

## Highlights

- Explore the dungeon with a party of up to four heroes, with shared adventures,
  cooperative magic, equipment, and ways to bring fallen companions back.
- Choose from new species including Automata, Hydras, Chain Devils, Tortles,
  Oozles, Eldritch, Faerie Dragons, and Meliai.
- Descend from the Lair into the Burrows, explore the Hive and Wormlands, and
  find another route to the Depths. The Forest and Hall of Blades also return.
- Worship Vorr, the Red Wake, or Vellum, the Archbinder, and discover new
  blood magic, auras, backgrounds, treasures, and encounters.
- Set up expeditions in a redesigned WebTiles lobby, with character previews,
  party controls, a game library, profiles, and expanded statistics.

## Multiplayer

- Create a party of up to four heroes. Each hero has their own species,
  background, equipment, skills, and religious allegiance.
- Choose classic friendly fire or allow allied projectiles and spells to pass
  safely through party members.
- Downed heroes can be revived by their companions, including through certain
  spells and items. Several existing spells can also aid or target allies.
- Trade with companions and use the party interface to inspect their status,
  skills, faith, progress, and initiative. Saved party compositions can be
  loaded again when preparing an expedition.
- Resume an existing game from another session, transferring the connection
  from the previous session.
- Multiplayer expeditions can find special cooperative spells, consumables,
  equipment, monsters, and vaults.

## Species

- **Automata** engrave spells into their bodies. Engraved spells fire
  automatically in response to events and generate heat. Automata can engrave
  a laser, and corrosion replaces ordinary mutation and magical contamination.
- **Chain Devils** use living chains to tether a foe, eventually learning to
  pull enemies close and pin them in place.
- **Eldritch** profane divine altars to steal gods' powers, accepting their
  wrath and strengthening the stolen benefits through later rites.
- **Ents** are slow, sturdy treefolk who can pass through walls and trees and
  wield most two-handed weapons alongside a shield.
- **Faerie Dragons** fly, cast spells more cheaply, and gain natural protection
  as they grow, but are conspicuous and cannot wear body armour.
- **Hermit Crabs** shelter in growing shells that can break under attack. They
  can molt to recover and scuttle away after losing a shell.
- **Hydras** grow additional heads and amulet capacity as they gain experience,
  but cannot use weapons or shields.
- **Irits** are armoured spellcasters who spend health as well as magic to
  cast, and whose magical shield shares incoming damage between HP and MP.
- **Kitsune** are nimble fox-folk who can charm hostile minds.
- **Meliai** are swift, frail bee-folk with innate faith and a smite that
  temporarily costs maximum health.
- **Oozles** are gelatinous creatures that assimilate properties from carried
  equipment into memories. They resist poison and corrosion but fear fire.
- **Proteans** gain health from mutations and change size as they grow, opening
  up different equipment choices.
- **Sparkborn** are immune to electricity and can retaliate with a blinkbolt;
  water interferes with their magic.
- **Tortles** are slow, heavily shelled swimmers who can retract and dash into
  enemies, and may bite attackers in retaliation.
- **Two-Headed Ogres** can wield two weapons and wear two amulets, but cannot
  use shields.
- **Wights** drain enemies in melee and can bind a weapon into their memories
  to enhance it at a temporary cost to maximum health.
- Several retired species return, including High Elves, Meteorans, Halflings,
  Hill Orcs, Sludge Elves, Centaurs, Deep Dwarves, Ghouls, Armataurs,
  Mayflytaurs, and Lava Orcs. High Elves have a skill floor based on experience
  level; Lava Orcs gain heat-themed defences and attacks.
- Eligible characters can choose Mummy, Zombie, or Ghost as an undead trait
  while retaining their species identity. Poltergeists, Revenants, and other
  nonliving species cannot select an undead trait. Mummy remains available as
  a legacy species for existing saves.

## Backgrounds

- **Paladins** serve the Shining One and begin with protective aura magic and
  an intervention that aids themselves and a nearby ally.
- **Blood Liturgists** serve Vorr and begin with a vampiric weapon and a library
  of blood-themed spells.
- **Healers, Death Knights, Abyssal Knights, Jesters, and Skalds** return as
  starting backgrounds, with their own equipment, skills, and magic or faith.

## Gods and magic

- **Vorr, the Red Wake**, rewards bloody combat. Fresh blood and corpses fuel
  rites that rupture foes, carry followers between sites of bloodshed, and
  raise blades from a battlefield. Followers can also spend their own health.
- **Vellum, the Archbinder**, lets followers extract enchantments from artefacts
  as threads and bind them to other compatible artefacts. Each thread is
  conserved, and the follower's capacity grows with devotion.
- **Pakellas** offers an inventor's workshop centred on a designated Prototype.
  Followers can overclock devices or borrow power against future recovery.
- **Dithmenos** has shadow-themed abilities, including Shadow Step and Shadow
  Form.
- The **Auramancy** skill strengthens sustained auras, including Protection,
  Zeal, Rime, Vitality, Sanctuary, Passage, and Resonance.
- **Kindled Veins, Winter's Due, and Borrowed Thunder** reserve maximum health
  to add fire, cold, or electricity to weapon strikes. **Scarlet Fetters**
  binds a quarry, while **Stolen Sight** impairs its attacks; both have
  health-costed amplified versions.
- **Linked Ward, Song of Fellowship, and Cleansing Mist** give parties new ways
  to protect, strengthen, and cleanse one another. Prayer of Fellowship aids
  nearby faithful companions.
- Fulsome Distillation, Evaporate, Controlled Blink, Regeneration, Shroud of
  Golubria, Song of Slaying, Conjure Flame, Cloud Cone, and Scattershot return
  or receive revisions.

## Branches and encounters

- **The Burrows** descend for four floors from the bottom of the Lair, through
  dens and tunnels inhabited by burrowing beasts and deep trolls.
- **The Hive** branches from Burrows:2. Its two floors contain bees, wasps,
  meliai, guarded treasure, and pools of honey that slow grounded movement.
- **The Wormlands** descend for four floors from the Burrows to the Depths,
  through galleries, mushroom groves, and the domains of worms and trolls.
  This route has no rune and does not replace the usual Dungeon entrance to
  the Depths.
- **The Forest** returns as a rune branch, and the **Hall of Blades** returns
  off the Vaults.
- New terrain, vaults, monsters, and rewards accompany the new routes. The
  balance of the full Burrows-to-Depths journey is still provisional.

## Items

- New cooperative potions include **empathy, soul ambrosia, concord, and
  transfusion**. New cooperative scrolls include **soul recall, rallying,
  phase swap, and shared vigour**.
- The **aegis standard, astral beacon, twin-spark conduit, and horn of the
  Vanguard** are experience-recharged devices for supporting a party.
- The **amulet of the vanguard** and **ring of camaraderie** reward fighting
  alongside companions. Multiplayer expeditions can also find additional
  cooperative artefacts.
- Potions of poison, strong poison, slowing, decay, and water return, with
  uses in the revised Evaporate and Fulsome Distillation spells.

## WebTiles and quality of life

- The redesigned lobby brings party setup, character previews, expedition
  navigation, and saved compositions together.
- A game library, Chronicle, profiles, appearance and graphics settings,
  site resources, and expanded statistics make it easier to revisit games and
  explore results.
- Categorized game settings support search, filtering customized values,
  per-setting reset, and undo.
- The game view adds party panels, richer tooltips, custom artwork, lighting,
  terrain presentation, and visual effects.
- Session recovery, input handling, connection stability, and auto-explore
  responsiveness have received improvements.

## Coming in the curated content port

*The following is the rest of the selected port scope. It is included for
planning the full release and should not be advertised as available until the
corresponding work is complete and verified.*

### Backgrounds and the Elder Pantheon

The Elder Pantheon has five birthright starts: Paladin for the Shining One,
Healer for Elyvilon, Berserker for Trog, the new **Preacher** for Zin, and the
new **Sage** for Sif Muna. Okawaru, Kikubaaqudgha, and Cheibriados are silent
elders, found through their altars.

- **Hermit:** begins with Ru and a sacrifice already made.
- **Slime Priest:** begins with Jiyva mutations.
- **Archaeologist:** starts with a whip and curare and pursues an artefact
  through an ancient crate and dusty tome.
- **Caravan:** starts with a pouch and enters the mercenary economy.
- **Collector:** begins with collection and wish scrolls.
- **Rogue:** combines an assassin's and artificer's tools.
- **Fencer:** starts with an acrobat's kit.
- **Ronin:** receives a piety bonus with the first god chosen.
- **Apothecary:** starts with homebrewed potions.
- **Billionaire:** relies on gold, stealth, and dodging.
- **Neckbeard:** carries a rusty katana and a teleport-behind trick.
- **Philosopher:** enters the dungeon with a thinking hat and little else.

### Gods and spells

- **Imus Thea, the Refracted** reflects projectiles; **the Great Wyrm**
  distils potions into essences; **Agraphede, the Primal Vermin** favours webs,
  traps, and poison.
- Six-star weapon blessings are planned for Elyvilon, Cheibriados, Zin, and
  Jiyva. Some gods will accept particular conversions without punishment.
- The spell selection includes **Shackle, False Image, Mana Rupture, Icicle
  Burst, Steam Barrier, Blight, Scorpion Form, Eringya's Rootspike, Olgreb's
  Last Mercy, Elemental Weapon, and Animate Armour**.

### Monsters and places

- **Cubus** disarms and strips jewellery, while the **interdimensional grape**
  melds equipment. Fire foxes and **Rin the nine-tailed** connect to Kitsune.
- The selected monsters also include the **Orb Demon, ironheart beastmaster,
  ancient automaton, greenwood witch, Asclepia, Brandagoth, Lichbane, and the
  Fallen**, along with a mercenary roster and killer bee larvae.
- **Golemforge** is a portal to a ruined coglin forge-temple, guarded by golems
  and crystal guardians and containing the four-piece Golem Armour set.
- Additional Slime Pits layouts and vaults, a Zot:5 hall, unrand vaults,
  mercenary shops, and selected Forest and Hive encounters are planned.

### Items and other changes

- New equipment includes the **ring of insulation, scarf of stasis, iron
  dragon scales, seeking bow brand, magic-recovery robe ego, and bunker shield
  ego**.
- Planned artefacts include **Fists of Thunder, Scorpio, Autumn Katana, Staff
  of Battle, sapphire dagger, butterfly sword, Viper's Fang, gauntlets of
  Distortion, scales of the Unseen Dragon, and Erysichthon's Jaw**.
- Utility items include the **pipe of recall, flask of healing mist, air horn,
  ancient crate, dusty tome, and scrolls of collection and wish**.
- Equippable **pouches, satchels, and bags of plenty** add inventory capacity;
  removing one while over capacity requires dropping items. Incorporeal
  species cannot carry one.
- Optional no-rune-lock and trap settings preserve essential puzzle passages.
  Further improvements include maces and flails knocking back corpses,
  weapon-damage displays, autofight weapon switching, and monster damage and
  spell information. The existing XP system remains unchanged.

The complete selection and design decisions are recorded in the
[port manifest](port-manifest.md). The Company Campaign and guild economy remain
separate design work.
