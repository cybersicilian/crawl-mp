What the artwork actually resolves

The Fugue zoom trio uses plus, minus and equals inside the magnifier. Inspect is a plain magnifier, camera is a camera and control is a hand, so those controls have distinct objects. Framed plus/cross/question symbols separate the three action-slot states. Brain and skull are distinct, but strength and quiver are missing rather than forced into lookalike hands or bottles.

Weak areas remain: footsteps and chain are dark on the HUD, the snowflake and ring are faint on paper, and the three flag variants have small secondary marks. “Abilities = bulb,” “skills = gavel,” “pickup = download tray,” and ownership arrows are metaphors, not self-evident fantasy actions. They need the existing labels/tooltips and recognition review. The compass cannot be replaced with Fugue's assembled compass because its needle must rotate independently. No source image was redrawn, recolored, synthesized or built by overlaying symbols.

These are isolated surface comparisons, not live-game screenshots. The HTML uses the actual --hud-button gradient from hud-polish.css and folio #f8f2e4 from tokens.css. The supplementary Pillow PNGs use a flat HUD midtone; the browser-rendered PNGs use the current gradient. Native item, spell, ability, deity and rune artwork remains separate.

Relationship to DCSS's project license

The repository's LICENSE identifies the program as GPL-2.0-or-later, most native tiles as CC0, and CC0 as the default for new artwork submissions. Keep candidate art as independently licensed PNG assets with its notices; do not relabel CC BY artwork as GPL or CC0. This recommendation is to distribute independent artwork alongside the GPL program, not to merge its terms into source-code licensing. CC explicitly permits separate game-art licensing; CC BY 3.0 sections 1(b) and 4(a) preserve a collection's separate licensing. GNU also lists CC BY 4.0 as GPL-compatible. Shade/CC0 best fits the stated default new-art policy; Fugue/Kyrise would need an explicit upstream policy exception if submitted upstream.

Distribution credits must name the artwork and author, link the source and exact license version, preserve notices and identify arrangement/cropping changes. The provided ATTRIBUTION.txt and original Fugue notice cover the local candidates. No attribution requirement was waived and no paid license was purchased.

No paid files obtained or used in comparisons. The older free pack is 32×32 and CC BY 4.0; do not transfer that license to the paid pack or downsample it as a native-16 substitute.
  • Pixel RPG Icons Adventure 16×16 — Thomas Feichtmeir (Cyangmou): $4.99 USD minimum. Creator permits free/commercial projects and edits but prohibits asset redistribution/resale. Excluded from this open-source import package; a purchase alone would not resolve repository rights. No purchase or files obtained.
  • 7Soul's RPG Graphics — Icons: $15.00 USD minimum; 1,700+ native 16px assets. Creator listing specifies CC BY-ND 4.0. The no-derivatives restriction is unsuitable for an unrestricted reusable art pipeline; not selected. Do not rely on conflicting third-party CC0 labels for older 7Soul icons.

Also screened: Kenney's Roguelike/RPG pack, free CC0, 1,700 16px tiles. Obtained and inspected, but its terrain/building emphasis and sparse relevant symbols made it weaker than the three finalists. AI-generated/code-rendered packs and monochrome vector families were excluded from this shortlist.

Review and import package

Import notes · 112-role mapping · 24 aliases · Archive URLs and SHA-256 provenance · Verification result · Partial-atlas manifest

The candidate folder contains 90 original-name PNGs and 90 role-name copies, plus a 256×112 atlas matching the existing 16-column canonical order. Every selected Fugue PNG is hash-identical to the author's file. All selected images are 16×16; atlas pixel comparisons pass; 22 transparent gap frames are explicitly declared. Alternatives are kept in their own comparison directory. Source archives and original licenses are retained.

37 metaphors require meaning review. The package sets complete=false and fallback=null; it must not silently replace the runtime atlas. A later integration can reuse the current frame order, alias table and icon wrapper while preserving tooltips, accessibility labels, exact stat values, compact layouts and native game artwork. No control behavior, deployment or runtime implementation was modified by this sourcing work.

Reproduce with python3 prepare.py (Pillow required), then python3 build_report.py. Art identity and coverage checks are bounded evidence. This is not a live-game render, accessibility certification or measured recognition study.
