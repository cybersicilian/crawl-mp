PROJECT LICENSE BOUNDARY
The DCSS program is GPL-2.0-or-later; most native artwork is CC0.
These independent candidate PNGs retain their own artwork licenses.
Do not relabel Fugue as GPL or CC0. Retain creator, title, source URL, license
link, notices and modification statement in distribution credits and alongside
the asset files. Do not add DRM or downstream redistribution restrictions.
For upstream submission, CC0 is the repository's stated usual new-art policy;
the CC BY candidates do not satisfy that default without a policy exception.

https://p.yusukekamiyamane.com/. Selected PNGs unchanged; arranged in an atlas."
Add Kyrise's CC BY 4.0 credit only if those assets are actually used.
