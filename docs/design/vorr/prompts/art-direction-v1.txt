Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one cohesive art direction board for a proposed Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup blood deity named Vorr the Red Wake; this is an editorial concept board, not a production sprite atlas.
Primary request: design the identity and assets for a god who rewards explosively bursting blood-bearing enemies, uses the spilled blood to fuel powerful rites, and grants passive regeneration and lifesteal at mid piety. Bloodless stone constructs must remain completely dry.
Format: polished landscape concept board, approximately 3:2. Charcoal-black background with restrained bone-white editorial headings. Exact large heading "VORR". Exact small subtitle "THE RED WAKE". Avoid all other small text and fake UI.
Composition: a large central altar study, a clear isolated insignia at the upper right, three smaller sharply pixelated ability studies along the bottom, and a small dungeon battle vignette at left. Keep each study distinct and generously spaced. Use thin muted dividing lines if helpful.
Altar subject: a compact low cracked dark basalt collecting basin seen from the slightly elevated frontal three-quarter perspective of classic Crawl 32x32 dungeon features. Black iron clamps, an ivory worn rim, four short channels cut through its rim, and a small suspended dark-red blood drop above the center. Deep crimson fluid, sharp scarlet highlights. It should feel heavy, ancient, and functional at tiny resolution.
Insignia: a single large blood drop split down the middle by a narrow negative-space cleft, with three short detached outward spatters. Bold, legible at 16 pixels. No letters.
Ability studies: Rupture is a blood drop bursting sharply outward; Blood Communion is red rivulets returning into a small dark adventurer silhouette at the chest; The Great Letting is low pools of blood rising as sharp inward-pointing blades around an enemy. These are visual studies within one board, not exported separate assets.
Battle vignette: an elevated square-grid dark stone dungeon. One armored adventurer stands in fresh glossy scarlet floor splashes, a fictional nonhuman monster has just burst in stylized red pixels, two distant stone golems are dry gray with absolutely no blood effects, cracks red only on blood-bearing effects, no realistic anatomy or organs.
Style: tightly controlled chunky pixel-art fantasy assets, deliberate clusters, black outlines, stepped shading, upper-left light, no smooth airbrushed gradients. Nearest-neighbor enlarged pixel studies against the professional art board. Floor details and monster silhouettes remain readable beneath blood. Fresh blood has sharp reflective points; dry blood is matte dark maroon.
Palette: soot #151116, basalt #383238, dried oxblood #4b1721, deep blood #8d1d32, fresh scarlet #d94950, bone #e6d9bb, dull iron #77707a.
Avoid: flames, demons, horns, skull emblems, vampire aristocracy, wings, ornate gold filigree, realistic gore, large anatomy, photorealism, exaggerated bloom, watermarks, production-ready claims.
