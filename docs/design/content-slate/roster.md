# Species and backgrounds

[Slate](README.md) · [Spells](spells.md) · [World](world.md) · [Delivery](plan.md)

All costs, durations, levels and progression below are prototype hypotheses. A normal action is 10 aut. Action time belongs to the owning floor; “turn” never means a teammate's click or wall-clock time. The starting species have ordinary movement speed unless later balance evidence warrants a change.

Revision, 22 October 2026: species should provide most enduring diversity; backgrounds remain starting templates. S5 Chain Devil replaces the former B2 Chainwarden background and is the next design focus. Concept IDs are preserved; B2 is retired rather than reassigned.

## S1 — Araneid

Spiderfolk with grasping arms and a spider abdomen. Their identity is shaping an approach, not faster movement or a general melee damage bonus.

- **Weave Silk:** spend 2 MP and one action to place a connected line of up to two web tiles within range three. At XL7 the line can contain three tiles. One weave exists per owner; a new successful weave replaces the old one. Lifetime: 60 aut, including the placement action.
- In the first design, silk is physical. Web-immune actors pass freely; other heroes can be caught too. Preview this risk and preserve native ally-harm conduct. Do not make all allies spiderlike by affiliation.
- Innate web immunity is an actor capability. Nets, constriction, poison, flight and climbing remain separate questions; the species receives none of those immunities implicitly.
- Normal weapons, shields and upper-body equipment fit. Boots do not; barding is not granted by analogy with centaurs. Confirm the existing slot policy before finalizing body armour fitting.
- Proposed strengths: Polearms, Hexes and Stealth. HP/MP start near human baseline. Full aptitudes wait for comparison with Merfolk, Naga, Formicid and Gale Centaur.
- Body-changing forms suppress silk anatomy according to the existing mutation/form contracts. Suppression prevents new weaves; existing silk obeys its recorded lifetime.

**Episode:** an approaching ogre can be intercepted at a doorway while the Araneid uses a polearm. A spider ignores the same route, making a direct fight, spell or retreat preferable. A teammate approaching through the doorway may make weaving it a bad choice.

**Smallest form:** one native web with ownership and expiry, then the short line. No web resource inventory, floor-spanning network or wall traversal. Later levels improve the existing action's reach or reliability only if tests justify it. The decisive failure is repeat placement trivializing melee pursuit.

## S2 — Myconid

Living fungal people who invest in a temporary colony. They use ordinary potions and healing; fungus does not imply undead status, universal poison immunity or special divine allegiance.

- **Sow Colony:** spend 2 MP and an action to place up to three nonblocking buds on selected legal floor cells around one visible center within range three. One colony per owner; maximum lifetime 70 aut.
- Buds mature once after 20 aut. They do not reproduce, generate resources, heal the owner or mature while their floor is paused.
- **Spore Bloom:** one action consumes the colony and emits a short physical spore burst around its surviving buds. Mature buds strengthen the burst. Prototype damage is subject to AC and one total per-target budget across overlapping buds; exact dice await combat tests.
- Collateral follows the spell/ability's explicit preview and normal conduct. No free pass through an ally standing in the burst. Buds do not block movement, stairs or sight.
- XL progression initially increases bloom effectiveness rather than the number of separately managed colonies. Anatomy and normal equipment remain broadly humanoid. Summoning and Alchemy are proposed aptitude strengths.

**Episode:** the Myconid sows near a bend and fights while the colony matures. A ranged threat refuses to approach, forcing an early small bloom or abandonment. The sunk investment does not prevent retreat.

**Distinction from Druid:** colony buds are one innate ground effect; Druid plants are attackable summoned actors. Forced Bloom does not multiply a Myconid's innate colony. Shared timing and ownership need not make the two object types interchangeable.

**Smallest form:** one bud with early/mature outcomes. Fire removal and other environmental reactions are later candidates, not assumed native rules. The decisive failure is routine empty-room preparation outperforming active combat choices.

## S3 — Glassborn

Living crystal bodies retain an element briefly, then choose between protection and discharge. They have normal equipment access; no Oozle-style property absorption.

- A damaging hostile fire, cold or electricity hit fills an empty facet with that element **after** the hit resolves. The triggering hit receives no new facet protection.
- One facet lasts 40 aut and supplies one native resistance pip for its element. Later hits neither replace nor refresh an occupied facet. Existing native resistance caps still apply.
- **Discharge Facet:** one action consumes the facet and fires a modest elemental bolt. Its power depends on XL, not damage endured. It cannot recharge itself or recursively trigger retaliation.
- Self-harm, allies, controlled summons and reflected damage from the owner cannot charge it. The attribution contract must follow the originating actor, not just the final projectile.
- Proposed baseline is slightly reduced HP with ordinary equipment and strong Conjurations/Alchemy potential. Quantify the HP cost only after measuring the defensive benefit.

**Episode:** fire protection against a caster competes with a fire discharge against another enemy. Against a fire-resistant foe, retaining the protection is sensible. A physical-only encounter supplies no facet.

**Smallest form:** fire only, one fixed charge, no stacking. Add cold/electricity after provenance and native resistance tests. Farming charges from trivial enemies is the principal exploit to assess.

## S4 — Echoborn

Living beings whose displaced image can serve as a temporary return point. Their identity is positional commitment, distinct from Poltergeist's undead body and equipment rules.

- **Shed Echo:** spend 3 MP and one action to step to a legal adjacent tile, leaving an obvious fragile decoy at the old position. One echo per owner, lasting 30 aut.
- **Exchange Echo:** spend one action to exchange positions once, requiring current sight, the same floor, legal destinations and normal translocation eligibility. Consume the echo on success; never rewind HP, MP, cooldowns, inventory or knowledge.
- Echoes do not attack, grant live sight, collect items, trigger loot or earn rewards. Enemies can destroy them. The owner cannot exchange through walls, onto an occupied tile or while teleportation is prohibited by the relevant native rule.
- Whether a decoy can be targetable without obstructing movement is an unresolved engine question. Keep the species behind the illusion experiment until that behavior is demonstrated or the design is revised explicitly.
- Proposed aptitude direction: Hexes, Translocations and Dodging. Use ordinary equipment and living physiology; no inherited insubstantial immunities.

**Episode:** leave an echo before stepping into a flank. Exchange back if it survives, or spend the remaining actions escaping when an enemy destroys it. Another hero occupying the return square can prevent the exchange.

**Smallest form:** one visible echo and one exchange. No attack cloning, repeated swaps or recursive echoes. A cheap guaranteed disengage in every fight is the principal balance failure.

## S5 — Chain Devil

A living devil whose bodily chains hold and reposition one foe. The working proposal is [detailed separately](chain-devil.md). Chain Lash, later Reel In and Pinning Chain are innate abilities with XL progression, not memorized spells or benefits of a Chainwarden class.

Use the same species with Fighter, Hunter, Conjurer or Warper starts. Its power must not require training a magic school or purchasing a chain weapon. Ordinary gear remains available; physical chains do not create extra wielding hands. One maintained relationship, action costs, a short recovery and the risk of holding a dangerous foe nearby are the initial constraints.

Demonic identity needs explicit native integration, including good-god/religious-background eligibility, forms and holy interactions. Do not copy Demonspawn's random mutations or assume a devil receives every demonic monster resistance. This remains a proposed species with no implemented or validated gameplay.

## Background packages

All four retained candidates are ordinary starting backgrounds with no exclusive passive, permanent class flag, required god or unlock tree. Each must justify its starting package; a new school-themed name alone is insufficient. Skills are draft starting levels before native species adjustment. A failed spell must still leave a viable weapon or consumable action. Starting spell memorization, Djinni behavior and equipment substitution require explicit native integration.

## Boundaries with existing options

| Existing option | Preserve its identity | New contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warper | Starting access to personal mobility and displacement spells | Chain Devil supplies a bodily tether to Warper or any other eligible starting template |
| Blood Liturgist | Starting access to HP commitments, weapon rites and quarry curses | Chain Devil's innate control changes geometry; combining it with Scarlet Fetters must not make an indefinite prison |
| Demonspawn | Random demonic mutation progression | Chain Devil has predictable bodily chain development; only the appropriate identity/eligibility mechanism is shared |
| Forgewright | Constructs and their combat patterns | Druid's stationary growth and one-shot blooming; avoid renaming a turret |
| Summoner | Broad mobile ally access | Druid commits to a location and sacrifices a plant's remaining life for a burst |
| Enchanter | Direct debuffs and stabbing opportunities | Illusionist changes targets and sound; illusion never automatically grants blindness stabs |
| Paladin / Skald | Existing auras, songs and frontline support | No new background-wide formation aura or mandatory healer role |
| Oozle | Equipment assimilation into a mutable body | Glassborn stores one temporary combat element, never item properties |
| Dithmenos | Divine shadow behavior and piety progression | Ordinary fragile illusions with smaller, explicit powers; no copied divine package |

Cross-build tests must include existing species using new books, new species using old backgrounds, and mixed co-op parties. The thematic combinations are examples, not eligibility rules.
