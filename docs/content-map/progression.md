## Reading a route correctly

Three things are different: **where an entrance occurs**, **what the game requires**, and **when a particular build should tackle it**. The routes here are conventional planning templates inferred from branch structure and threat/resource systems. No current player-route frequency study was performed.

“Normal length” means an Orb escape with three runes. It does not mean every floor must be cleared. “Extended” means optional exploration beyond that objective, conventionally up to 15 runes; Ziggurats are another axis and award no rune. XL ranges below are broad teaching bands, not entry requirements or balance measurements.

The local source includes modern trunk content, revived content and cooperative mechanics while the executable reports `0.33-a0`. That string is not a reliable content-version boundary. For this map, the checkout is authoritative for authored content; the executable export is evidence of compiled catalogue visibility only.

## Traditional three-rune routes

### A. Familiar all-round route

`D:1–Lair entrance → Lair:1-5 → Orc:1-2 → remaining suitable Dungeon → first S-branch → second S-branch → Vaults:1-4 / Depths → Vaults:5 → Zot:1-5 → Orb escape`

Lair and Orc order is flexible. Finish the easier available rune branch first, revisiting other areas when the current one is too dangerous. D:15, Depths, Vaults:1-4 and optional loot branches can be interleaved; the string is not a script for clearing everything.

The two Lair branches are one of **Swamp/Shoals** and one of **Snake/Spider**. Their endings supply the first two conventional runes. Vaults:5 supplies the silver third rune in this route. Entering V:5 is a separate commitment from clearing V:1-4.

### B. Slime third rune

Use the same early spine, prepare sufficiently in Vaults/Depths and optional areas, then `Slime:1-5 → Zot → escape`.

This swaps Vaults:5's concentrated arrival problem for Slime's acid, mutation and boss/terrain problem. In this checkout, descending below Slime:1 commits the player: upward travel is blocked until the ruling power is destroyed or joined. Jiyva changes that relationship. This is already a meaningful strategic commitment, not an empty region needing a new lock.

### C. Abyss third rune

Use the early spine, prepare an appropriate survival/mobility plan, then `Abyss → abyssal rune → return → Zot → escape`.

The Abyss is also an involuntary banishment/recovery environment. The guaranteed entrance is in late Dungeon in this source, unlike older route descriptions that place it in Depths. Entry location does not make it an early recommendation. Rune search has exploration/depth rules; fleeing and finding the rune are different objectives. Do not assume it is the universal easiest third rune.

### D. Fork-specific Forest alternative

`one or two S runes → Vaults:2-3 entrance → Forest:1-4 → mossy rune → remaining preparation → Zot`

This is **source-backed authored content and a compiled branch record**, not a behaviorally verified recommended route. Forest is no longer excluded by `branch_is_unfinished`, its branch record assigns `RUNE_FOREST`, and `forest_heart_of_arcadia` places the mossy rune. Generation, endings, enemy tuning, rune accounting and multiplayer travel still need a controlled native pass.

It could widen the third-rune choice or supply a five/six-rune middle game. It could also become an automatic easy pick that displaces existing branches. Decide its role before further expansion.

### E. High-commitment nonstandard routes

This source permits entering Vaults without a rune after a consequential confirmation, then prevents leaving through its exit without a rune. Thus **“one rune required to enter Vaults” is not the current contract**. A source-backed possibility is to seek a rune inside its connected region, but this is not a beginner recommendation or a route proven here.

God choices, banishment, species movement constraints and unusual finds can all change good routing. The map preserves these escape hatches; it does not prescribe one build or route as the correct way to play.

## Extended routes and useful stopping points

### Three → five runes

After two S runes, the remaining conventional ordinary options are **Vaults, Slime and Abyss**. Collecting all five provides a substantial intermediate goal without committing to every extended realm. Order follows build readiness and risk. This fork's Forest is an additional, separately qualified option.

### Five → fifteen runes

Traditional accounting for one normal dungeon:

| Rune family | Count available in one run | Relationship to progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generated water S-branch | 1 | Swamp OR Shoals |
| Generated poison S-branch | 1 | Snake OR Spider |
| Vaults, Slime, Abyss | 3 | Three distinct optional/ordinary rune sources |
| Four Hells | 4 | Iron, obsidian, icy and bone |
| Pandemonium | 5 | Demonic plus four named lord runes |
| Tomb | 1 | Golden rune |
| Total | 15 | Includes the first three; not 15 additional runes |

Traditional route family: `ordinary five + preparation → choose an appropriate first extended realm → interleave Pan/Hells as build/resources warrant → Tomb when ready → remaining goals → Zot/Orb escape`.

There is no universally safe Pan-before-Hell order. The four Hells also have different recovery/equipment restrictions, so “Hell” is not a single resistance check. Tomb's concentrated torment, smiting and death-trigger pressure makes it a distinct preparation problem, frequently approached late; it is not mechanically required to be last.

Clearing Zot before extended can provide experience, equipment and a prepared finale. **Taking the Orb** changes the expedition into an escape and is a separate decision. Do not imply a post-victory open world: escaping with the Orb ends the native run.

### Ziggurats and repeat challenges

Ziggurats are optional 27-floor, escalating crowd encounters with exit decisions. They are not needed for three, five or fifteen runes, and are not merely the next numbered branch after Tomb. Preparation and viable builds differ from ordinary extended play. Repeat-Ziggurat ambitions deserve separate goals and records so extreme optimization does not define the rest of the game.

### The fork's rune-count frontier

Forest adds a source-backed sixteenth candidate to the traditional fifteen, but `MAX_RUNES` remains 15 and normal initialization uses it for `you.obtainable_runes`. This is a concrete accounting inconsistency to investigate, not proof of a playable sixteen-rune end state. Check completion messages, score bonuses, species/background special cases, records, save/resume and any “all runes” predicates before advertising an all-rune fork challenge.

## Pacing rule for expansion

Add new content as **alternative vaults, objectives, endings or optional short operations before adding mandatory floors**. A three-rune win should remain a complete experience. Extended should add new decisions after build maturity. Repeating the same resistances and damage rotations across more floors is quantity, not a new progression layer.
