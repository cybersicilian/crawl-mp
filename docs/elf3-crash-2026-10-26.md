## Cause and repair

Production crashes and the user-provided `crash-report-2.txt` identify Elf:3,
`layout_geoelf_castle`, seed `11437096116346730075`, and destruction of damaged
`map_lines` strings. The primary vault was `grunt_elf_hall_asides`.

An encompass layout is centered in the 120×104 native grid, while a solo layout
allocates 80×70 cells. Vault bounds and masks use native grid coordinates;
castle drawing uses local coordinates. The original layout mixed the two and
could pass coordinates outside its string storage to unchecked native drawing
primitives.

The repair adds a reusable centered coordinate frame, translates primary-vault
bounds into local coordinates, and translates mask queries back to native
coordinates. It also vetoes a castle when its primary vault extends outside
that local grid, allowing another compatible layout. This additional constraint
was required after production QA found the same unsafe mechanism with
`minmay_elf_hall_furrowed`. Compatible castle layouts remain available, including
the full-size identity case. No map dimensions or character rules changed.
