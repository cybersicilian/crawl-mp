%%%%
Air Elementalist

Air Elementalists have learned the Shock spell. Their electrical magic can harm
groups of foes, while their air spells assist in fighting or fleeing enemies.
%%%%
Artificer

Artificers start with a few magic wands and a club. They'll need to find better
weapons and armour before their wands run out.
%%%%
Berserker

Berserkers believe in Trog, the magic-hating god of frenzy. Not much can stop a
raging berserker early on, apart from hubris. Berserkers are the birthright
order of Trog, one of the eight gods of the Elder Pantheon.
%%%%
Brigand

Brigands carry poisoned darts and a small number of nasty curare darts.
%%%%
Chaos Knight

Chaos Knights are playthings of the unpredictable Xom, and are subject to the
god's constantly changing moods.
%%%%
Cinder Acolyte

Cinder Acolytes worship Ignis, drawing upon the flame of a dying god until
there is nothing left. They have both melee and fire magic capabilities.
%%%%
Conjurer

Conjurers begin with the Magic Dart spell, and can learn other non-elemental
spells to reliably blast their foes.
%%%%
Delver

Delvers start their quest five levels below the surface. A set of powerful magic
items helps them survive the dangerous journey upward.
%%%%
Earth Elementalist

Earth Elementalists know the Sandblast spell, and can later learn more earthen
spells suitable for subtle stabbing or brute-force blasting.
%%%%
Enchanter

Enchanters know a variety of Hexes that can incapacitate their foes, and are
equipped with an enchanted dagger and potions of invisibility.
%%%%
Fighter

Fighters are equipped with armour and shield, as well as a weapon of their
choice and potions of might.
%%%%
Fire Elementalist

Fire Elementalists have learned the Foxfire spell. Their other starting spells
are likewise focused on fiery and explosive destruction.
%%%%
Gladiator

Gladiators are ready for the arena with light armour, a weapon of their choice,
and a few throwing weapons and nets.
%%%%
Hedge Wizard

Hedge Wizards are magic generalists, good at using a wide variety of tools to
frustrate their foes and gain an edge in the battlefield.
%%%%
Hunter

Hunters carry a shortbow, light armour and a scroll of butterflies.

%%%%
Hexslinger

Hexslingers carry a sling, and use hex magic to make their foes more vulnerable
to attacks from afar.
%%%%
Ice Elementalist

Ice Elementalists begin with the Freeze spell. Their later spells provide strong
offence and defence, but may restrict their movement.
%%%%
Monk

Monks start with a simple weapon of their choice, an orb of light, a potion
of divine ambrosia, a potion of enlightenment, and gain additional piety with
the first god they worship.
%%%%
Mystic

Mystics transform their bodies with talismans to be more attuned with magic.
Their spells require mobility in combat, but can tap into all four elements.
%%%%
Necromancer

Necromancers start out with the Soul Splinter spell. Eventually, they can turn
the dead into allies and use the life force of the living to restore themselves.
%%%%
Blood Liturgist

Blood Liturgists worship Vorr, the Red Wake. They enter the dungeon with a
chosen vampiric weapon, leather armour, a potion of curing and a scroll of fog. They
know Kindled Veins and have Winter's Due, Borrowed Thunder, Scarlet Fetters
and Stolen Sight in their spell library. Their training balances weapon combat,
survival, Necromancy and Hexes. Anyone may learn these spells, and a Blood
Liturgist may freely develop another style.
%%%%
Reaver

Reavers start with a weapon of their choice and deadly magic to supplement it
when in need. They start with the Kiss of Death spell.
%%%%
Summoner

Summoners are able to cast Summon Small Mammal from the start, and begin with
spells to summon stronger and more exotic allies in their library.
%%%%
Forgewright

Forgewrights are mages who specialise in the creation of turrets and mechanical
companions to fight alongside.
%%%%
Shapeshifter

Shapeshifters use talismans to shift their form, making them powerful melee
fighters (especially when unarmed) at the cost of armour.
%%%%
Stalker

Stalkers transform their bodies with talismans to better deceive and debilitate
their enemies. Eventually they can even transform potions into sprays of harmful
clouds.
%%%%
Alchemist

Alchemists start with the Poisonous Vapours spell, and their magic is very
effective against foes susceptible to poison.
%%%%
Wanderer

Wanderers start with random equipment and skills.
%%%%
Warper

Warpers start with a weapon of their choice, a scroll of blinking, darts of
disjunction, and some Translocation spells in their library.
%%%%
Healer

Healers are devoted followers of Elyvilon, the Healer, starting with healing
potions and divine abilities to mend wounds, purify ailments, and protect their
allies. Healers are the birthright order of Elyvilon, one of the eight gods of
the Elder Pantheon.
%%%%
Death Knight

Death Knights serve Yredelemnul the Dark, marching into battle in heavy armour
to harvest souls and lead an undying host alongside their companions.
%%%%
Skald

Skalds are warrior-poets who weave battle-songs and enchantments, empowering
themselves and their allies with Song of Slaying, Shroud of Golubria, and
Regeneration.
%%%%
Jester

Jesters are manic, capering servants of Xom who enter the dungeon wielding an
enchanted chaos-branded quarterstaff.
%%%%
Paladin

Paladins are holy knights of The Shining One, combining a chosen weapon, ring
mail and buckler with supportive magic. They begin with Aura of Protection
learned and a library containing Protection, Zeal, Rime, Vitality and Sanctuary.
Each caster sustains one aura at a time, with range and strength improved by
Auramancy. Clear line of sight is required. Different auras combine; copies use
only the strongest caster's power. Lay on Hands & Intervene heals and briefly
wards the Paladin and a nearby ally, costs 3 MP, scales with Invocations, and
requires 30 turns of recovery. This invocation requires continued service to
The Shining One. Demigods, undead and demonspawn cannot become Paladins.
Paladins are the birthright order of the Shining One, one of the eight gods of
the Elder Pantheon.
%%%%
Preacher

Preachers enter the dungeon in Zin's service with a chosen weapon, a robe and
silver javelins. They train stealth and invocations. Demigods, undead and
demonspawn cannot become Preachers; undead traits are also incompatible.
Preachers are the birthright order of Zin, one of the eight gods of the Elder
Pantheon.
%%%%
Sage

Sages serve Sif Muna and begin with Apportation and the contents of a random
spellbook in their library. They wear a robe, carry a dagger and rely on their
knowledge of spellcasting. Demigods cannot become Sages. Sages are the
birthright order of Sif Muna, one of the eight gods of the Elder Pantheon.
%%%%
Abyssal Knight

Abyssal Knights serve Lugonu the Unformed, entering the dungeon with a rift-touched
weapon, leather armour, and dimensional invocations to bend and corrupt space.
%%%%
Hermit

Hermits begin as followers of Ru, having already sacrificed a part of
themselves for power. Their path is a special devotion, not a birthright order.
%%%%
Slime Priest

Slime Priests enter the dungeon as followers of Jiyva, already changed by the
slime god's mutations.
%%%%
Archaeologist

Archaeologists carry a whip, curare darts, a dusty tome and a sealed crate.
At experience level 3 they can decipher the tome and train the named skill;
after 700 bonus training points, their crate reveals a reserved ancient relic.
Inspect the tome or crate to see the owner's progress.
%%%%
Rogue

Rogues combine stealth and poisoned weapons with magical devices. They begin
with a +2 dagger, robe, cloak, poisoned darts, and wands of flame and charming.
%%%%
Fencer

Fencers begin as nimble duelists with a rapier, fencer's gloves and an amulet
of the acrobat. The gloves enable ripostes; the amulet rewards movement. If
their gloves are already reserved by the party, they receive ordinary gloves.
%%%%
Ronin

Ronin enter the dungeon without a god. Their first faith grants extra piety,
or a faster first sacrifice for Ru; Gozag instead halves the first joining fee.
%%%%
Apothecary

Apothecaries venture into the dungeon with a selection of homebrewed potions
and the hat of the Alchemist. If their hat is already reserved by the party,
they receive an ordinary hat.
%%%%
Billionaire

Billionaires enter the dungeon in a bathrobe with one million gold, relying on
stealth and evasiveness to protect their fortune. A party receives this shared
fortune once, even if more than one member is a Billionaire.
%%%%
Neckbeard

Neckbeards carry a battered blade and can Step Behind a visible enemy for 2 MP.
The move shares Blink's cooldown and obeys translocation restrictions.
%%%%
Philosopher

Philosophers enter the dungeon with the hat of Pondering and little else,
relying on their intellect to make the most of a difficult start. If their hat
is already reserved by the party, they receive an ordinary hat.
%%%%
Caravan

Caravans venture into the Dungeon accompanied by a hired mercenary. They
start with extra gold, a pouch, a pipe to recall allies and a flask of healing
mist to help sustain their party.
%%%%
Collector

Collectors begin with a weapon, light clothing and a scroll of Collector's
Choice. When read, it offers a choice between a collection of artefacts and a
wish for an available named or random artefact. The scroll is not spent when
the reader cancels.
%%%%
