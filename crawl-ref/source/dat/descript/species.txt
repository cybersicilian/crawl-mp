%%%%
Meliai

Meliai are the winged bee-folk of the Hive. Swift but frail, they are deeply
faithful and can call down a smite at the cost of temporary maximum health.
Their sting is venomous, but they are vulnerable to poison themselves.
%%%%
Kitsune

Kitsune are nimble fox-folk with an affinity for subtle magic. They can
charm hostile minds in sight, but must recover from the effort before doing so
again. Their fox ears prevent them from wearing headgear. Rin the nine-tailed
is a renowned Kitsune whose power is spoken of
among her people.
%%%%
Automaton

Automata are magical constructs whose spells are engraved into their bodies.
Their mechanisms build heat as they unleash magic automatically.
They use heat instead of mana: magic regeneration improves heat dissipation,
magic restoration cools them, and magical power increases their heat capacity.
Guardian spirit absorbs damage by building heat in place of spending mana.
Offensive and summoning engravings can activate after your actions when a
visible threat is present, including moving or waiting; no weapon attack is
required. Each spell still needs a safe target or effect and time to cool.
Mutagenic effects and magical contamination corrode their bodies instead of
changing them or causing magical glow.
Each spell's description explains its automatic trigger. Some spells have no
automatic trigger and will not fire when engraved.
At experience level 5, automata discover Engraved Laser in their spell library.
They can learn this level 6 pattern from experience level 6, after which it can
fire automatically.
%%%%
Wight

Wights are ancient warriors animated by negative energy. As they gain
experience their melee attacks drain living enemies, and they can bind their
memories to a weapon to strengthen it, temporarily draining their maximum
health until they gain more experience.
%%%%
Sparkborn

Sparkborn are distant kin of raiju, with living lightning coursing through
their bodies. They are immune to electricity, but water attacks drain their
magic and grounded water prevents spellcasting. Injury can release a blinkbolt,
letting them choose a nearby destination and shock creatures along the way.
Suppressing this reaction causes magical contamination.
%%%%
Irit

Irits are armoured spellcasters who spend their own health as well as magic to
cast. Armour and shields impede their lower-level spells less, and their
bloodcraft requires less life. A magical shield shares incoming damage between
their health and magic reserves.
%%%%
Hermit Crab

Hermit Crabs are clever crustaceans who shelter in shells that grow with them.
When a shell breaks, they can escape and find a smaller one. Their molting and
sideways movement reflect their crustacean heritage.
%%%%
Ent

Ents are ancient, slow-moving treefolk protected by thickening bark. Their
branches can wield most two-handed weapons alongside a shield, and they can
pass through walls and trees. Their rooted bodies cannot wear body armour or boots and
cannot change form.
%%%%
Faerie Dragon

Faerie Dragons are small winged spellcasters. Their spells cost less magic,
and their bright scales provide armour and shielding as they grow, at the cost
of stealth. While flying, they gain 10% evasion, with a minimum bonus of 1 EV.
Their wings prevent them from wearing body armour.
%%%%
Protean

Proteans are amoeboid people whose acquired mutations increase their health.
Their maximum health determines their size, and their protean flesh cannot
change form. They begin too small to wield weapons or wear most armour, but
can use larger equipment as their bodies grow.
%%%%
Hydra

Hydras grow heads as they gain experience. They gain a bite against their
chosen target and an amulet slot at one head, and another of each at four,
seven, ten heads, and so on. Their remaining heads may bite adjacent foes.
Their bodies cannot wield weapons or carry shields.
%%%%
Two-Headed Ogre

Two-headed ogres are powerful, noisy fighters. Each head can wear an amulet,
and their two hands can wield weapons together, but they cannot use shields.
%%%%
Barachi

Barachim are slow-moving, amphibious humanoids who can hop long distances.
Their extended range of vision also allows them to be seen from further away.
%%%%
Coglin

Coglins ride enchanted exoskeletons able to wield weapons in both hands,
wrapping slow-revving metal around their puny goblinoid frames.
%%%%
Deep Elf

Deep Elves have a strong affinity for all kinds of magic, but are very fragile.
They regenerate magical power rapidly.
%%%%
Demigod

Demigods refuse to worship any god, but make up for this with divine attributes
and high reserves of health and magic.
%%%%
Demonspawn

Demonspawn are half human, half demon. They acquire strong demonic mutations as
they grow.
%%%%
Djinni

Djinn are ever-burning spirits. They learn spells automatically as they grow,
and cast by drawing down their own fires.
%%%%
Draconian

Draconians are versatile hybrids. They mature when reaching level 7 and develop
a colour.
%%%%
Formicid

Formicids are humanoid ants adept at digging. Their limbs are exceptionally
strong and they live in a state of permanent stasis. Their four arms allow them
to wear two pairs of gloves and four rings.
%%%%
Felid

Felids are many-lived sentient cats. They cannot use armour or weapons.
%%%%
Gale Centaur

Powerful equine hybrids who trace their ancestry to the four winds themselves,
Gale Centaurs are at their strongest while galloping swiftly towards foes. They
can wear two pairs of boots as well as barding.
%%%%
Gargoyle

Made of living stone, Gargoyles have a preternatural affinity for earth magic.
They have low health, but their stone bodies are incredibly tough.
%%%%
Gnoll

Gnolls are canine-like humanoids. They are unable to specialise in any single
skill, but instead gradually become proficient in all skills at once.
%%%%
Human

Humans are natural explorers. Discovering new places refreshes and invigorates them.
They are otherwise completely average.
%%%%
Tengu

Tengu are bird-people who love to fight, both with weapons and spells. They are
fragile but agile, and eventually learn to fly.
%%%%
Kobold

Kobolds are attuned to the shadows and can only see or be seen at a close range.
They are small and a little fragile, and are talented in most skills.
%%%%
Merfolk

Half fish, half human, Merfolk are citizens of both water and land. They are
strong combatants, and adept at using magic to assist in battle.
%%%%
Minotaur

Minotaurs are large muscular humans with bovine heads. They excel at all forms
of close and ranged combat.
%%%%
Mountain Dwarf

Mountain Dwarves are strong melee fighters and capable heavy-armoured casters.
They can use scrolls to enchant even some artefacts.
%%%%
Mummy

Compelled to walk by an ancient curse, Mummies have innate faith and are adept
at Necromancy, but learn other skills very slowly. They cannot drink potions.
%%%%
Naga

Nagas are hybrids: human from the waist up, with a snake-like tail instead of
legs. They move very slowly but are naturally resilient.
%%%%
Octopode

Octopodes are a species of amphibious cephalopods. They can wear eight rings,
but almost no armour fits them.
%%%%
Oozle

Oozles are living, translucent oozes with amber nuclei and temporary pseudopods.
Their gelatinous bodies grow more protective with experience, resist poison
and corrosion, and slip through nets, webs and constricting coils. They can
wear hats and scarves, hold weapons and shields, and suspend ordinary jewellery
within their gel. Their light bodies have reduced health and are vulnerable
to fire. They deliberately assimilate carried equipment, preserving extracted
property bundles in random empty memory slots. Worn gear never dissolves
passively. All absorbed properties strengthen the ooze itself,
independently of equipped items. Weapon memories can improve natural unarmed
attacks, including cleaving. Shapeshifting
increases the strength of numeric memory bonuses. They are especially adept
at Alchemy, but learn all manufactured-weapon skills poorly.
%%%%
Oni

Large, robust, and extremely fond of drinking, Oni make capable brawlers and
spellcasters and gain extra benefits from healing potions.
%%%%
Poltergeist

Formless ghosts who delight in hexing their enemies. They can equip many
different hats, gloves, boots, or cloaks at once, but cannot wear body armour.
%%%%
Revenant

Revenants are skeletal undead who are strong at melee combat and can empower
damage-dealing spells by consuming memories they harvest from their victims.
%%%%
Spriggan

Very small, very frail and very fast, Spriggans are highly competent stabbers
and spellcasters.
%%%%
Tortle

Large, cold-blooded turtle folk with heavy shells that grow tougher as they
mature. They move slowly and fit into little conventional armour, but excel
with shields and can swim through deep water without gaining speed or agility.
They can retract into their shells, then launch themselves in a direction
until blocked. Shell impacts grow stronger with level, Unarmed Combat, and
distance travelled.
When struck in melee, they may reflexively bite their attacker, with damage
that grows with level and Unarmed Combat.
%%%%
Troll

Trolls are monstrous creatures with powerful claws. They regenerate health
rapidly.
%%%%
Vine Stalker

Frail symbionts, Vine Stalkers cannot regain health from potions. Their magical
reserves absorb damage, and they pack a magic-restoring bite.
%%%%
Meteoran

Star-born beings of immense talent who burn brightly and briefly. They excel at
almost all skills and regenerate by exploring new territory, though the Zot
clock ticks faster for them.
%%%%
Halfling

Small, nimble folk who excel with short blades, slings, and ranged weapons.
They are naturally resistant to mutagenic effects.
%%%%
Hill Orc

Stout, hardy warriors with an affinity for axes, heavy armour, and divine
invocations.
%%%%
High Elf

High Elves cannot train most skills below one third of their experience level.
These graceful elves excel with blades, bows, and air magic. The training
threshold is rounded down; Fighting, Spellcasting and Invocations are exempt.
%%%%
Sludge Elf

Adaptable elves attuned to primal magic, shapeshifting, and unarmed combat.
%%%%
Centaur

Swift four-legged equines with natural toughness and mastery of ranged weapons.
%%%%
Deep Dwarf

Subterranean dwarves who do not regenerate health naturally, but shrug off
damage and can mend their own and adjacent allies' wounds by channeling magic.
%%%%
Ghoul

Undead horrors with vicious claws and resilience to negative energy and torment.
They mend their wounds by devouring the life force of slain foes.
%%%%
Armataur

Large, heavily-plated mammals that wear barding and gain momentum as they roll
toward their foes.
%%%%
Mayflytaur

Winged, ephemeral beings gifted with extraordinary skill aptitudes and passive
mapping, driven ever onward by their brief lifespan.
%%%%
Lava Orc

Volcanic orcs whose molten scales harden as they grow and whose searing blood
splashes magma onto foes in melee while warming nearby party members with cold
resistance (rC+).
%%%%
%%%%
Chain Devil

Chain Devils bear living iron in their flesh. Their chains strike a foe at a distance, tether it, draw it close at XL7 and fasten it to the dungeon at XL13. A devil maintains one chain at a time and must recover after releasing it. The chains neither silence nor constrict their quarry. Ordinary weapons, armour and magic remain available, while demonic nature bars worship of the good gods.
%%%%
Eldritch

Eldritch worship no god. Instead they can profane up to three gods' own altars, permanently stealing one benefit from each and enduring its wrath. Their shared Defiance is replenished by ordinary combat experience. Elder Rites at XL9, XL15 and XL21 strengthen those same benefits through short fights with unrewarding trial monsters. They are living, blood-bearing humanoids who use ordinary equipment and receive ordinary stat growth.
%%%%
Mummy trait

A deathless body bound by an ancient curse. Mummies resist cold, negative
energy, poison and torment, but are vulnerable to fire and cannot drink
potions. Their innate faith aids worship, and their Necromancy is enhanced,
with a further enhancement at experience level 13.
%%%%
Zombie trait

An animated dead body retains its species' anatomy and equipment, but cannot
heal naturally while threatened by nearby enemies. Zombies resist cold,
negative energy, poison and torment.
%%%%
Ghost trait

A spectral remnant retains the abilities and equipment of its host species.
Its insubstantial body resists certain hazards, and it resists negative energy,
poison and torment. It does not inherit the Poltergeist's equipment-haunting
abilities.
