✦Pebble Broth · Cooperative tactics roguelike
A 1–4 hero tactical dungeon crawl

Gather your party.
Descend together.

Each player commands one hero. Your party shares every explored floor, its gold and its spell library, then fights on an initiative clock where banked time and recovery debt decide who moves next. Choose your friendly-fire rules, take today's seed, and see how deep four heroes can go.

  • Party of 1–4 heroesFriends, companions you steer, or Jev AI players filling the empty seats.
  • Initiative-based tactical combatBank time, pay recovery debt, and queue a plan before you commit.
  • Shared map, personal perceptionExplored floors, gold and spells are pooled; what each hero sees is their own.
  • Daily seeded expeditionOne dungeon per day for every party, with friendly fire set to classic or safe.
